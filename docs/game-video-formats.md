# Game video format feasibility

Researched 2026-11-09 using vendor documentation and upstream source. This is an engineering feasibility assessment, not a list of supported GameZip replacements. No game files were changed; no Bink, Smacker, USM or SFD encoding tests were performed.

## What works today

GameZip creates a **separate 1080p preview** from a standalone 3840x2160 H.264, 8-bit, constant-frame-rate MP4 meeting its restricted profile. It does not replace installed-game videos. Other formats below require implementation and game/build-specific playback validation. See [the implementation checkpoint](video-pipeline.md) and [Cyberpunk inspection](cyberpunk-video-compatibility.md).

The checksum-verified local FFmpeg 9.0.2 essentials build exposes libx264, libx265, libvpx, libvpx-vp9, libtheora, mpeg1video, mpeg2video, wmv1, wmv2 and roqvideo encoders; its encoder listing has no Bink, Smacker or VC-1 encoder. This establishes tool capabilities only. Upstream [codec registrations](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavcodec/allcodecs.c) distinguish encoders from decoders; a container muxer is not a video encoder.

## Format candidates

| Files | Decode and resize feasibility | Output usable by the original game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `.mp4`, `.m4v` | H.264 is the strongest initial candidate; H.265/other codecs require separate profiles. | Preserve the game's accepted codec/profile, audio, timing and container details. Unreal recommends H.264 MP4, but playback depends on the selected player and OS codecs. [Epic](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/media-framework-technical-reference-for-unreal-engine) |
| `.webm` | VP8/VP9 video can be decoded, resized and re-encoded with suitable FFmpeg builds. | Match the actual game: Unity usually imports VP8 + Vorbis; generic WebM also permits VP9 and Opus. Valid WebM alone does not prove runtime support. [Unity](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/video/sources/file-compatibility), [WebM specification](https://www.webmproject.org/docs/container/) |
| `.avi`, `.mov`, `.mkv` | These identify containers; inspect their streams first. Encoding feasibility depends on the codec inside. | Keep the game's codec and features. MOV/HAP may carry alpha; converting transparent video to ordinary H.264 can lose its function. MKV is not a universal game playback target. [FFmpeg format/codec tables](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/doc/general_contents.texi), [Epic HAP support](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/media-framework-technical-reference-for-unreal-engine#hapcodecplaybacksupport) |
| `.wmv`, `.asf` | Decoding a source does not guarantee its codec can be re-encoded. | Local wmv1/wmv2 encoders do not produce WMV3/VC-1. A game requiring those needs an independently established encoder path. [FFmpeg codec registrations](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavcodec/allcodecs.c) |
| `.mpg`, `.mpeg`; `.ogv` | MPEG-1/2 and Theora have encoding paths in the local build. | Potential future profiles must preserve the expected container, audio and timing. Unity lists these imports with platform differences; this does not certify installed-game replacement. [Unity](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/video/sources/file-compatibility), [FFmpeg tables](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/doc/general_contents.texi) |
| `.bik` Bink 1 | FFmpeg supports Bink 1 decoding, but no Bink encoder. | RAD's encoder offers Bink 1 output and scaling. Exact revision/runtime compatibility still needs validation. [Bink demuxer](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavformat/bink.c), [RAD compression help](https://www.radgametools.com/binkhcwb.htm) |
| `.bk2` Bink 2 | **FFmpeg recognizing headers is not video decoding:** its demuxer explicitly reports Bink 2 video unimplemented. | RAD tools document Bink 2/HDR output and scaling. Access, licensing and compatibility with the shipped decoder must be established; renaming Bink 1 output is not proof of compatibility. [FFmpeg source](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavformat/bink.c), [RAD](https://www.radgametools.com/binkhcwb.htm) |
| `.usm` CRI Sofdec | FFmpeg's USM reader maps MPEG-1, H.264 and VP9 plus ADX/HCA audio. It does not establish support for every USM variant or proprietary Sofdec.Prime. | CRI's encoder supports resolution overrides and USM packaging, cue points, languages, subtitles and alpha-related options. Preserve these; an ordinary MP4 is not equivalent. [FFmpeg USM reader](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavformat/usmdec.c), [CRI wizard](https://blog.criware.com/index.php/2024/12/23/the-sofdec-encoding-wizard/) |
| `.sfd` legacy Sofdec | FFmpeg's MPEG reader has explicit Sofdec handling, including ADX audio. Validate individual files. | Generic MPEG encoding/muxing does not certify the original Sofdec layout. A supported legacy authoring/repack path remains unresolved. [FFmpeg MPEG reader](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavformat/mpeg.c) |
| `.smk` Smacker | FFmpeg decoding; no upstream Smacker encoder. | RAD documents legacy 256-colour encoding and scaling. Encoder availability does not establish redistribution rights or game compatibility. [FFmpeg tables](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/doc/general_contents.texi), [RAD Smacker help](https://www.radgametools.com/binkhcws.htm) |
| `.roq`; `.thp` | RoQ has FFmpeg decode/encode and mux support. THP has decoding/demuxing, without an upstream THP output path in the cited table. | RoQ needs game-specific constraints/testing; THP needs a separately validated authoring path. Low priority until actual eligible high-resolution sources are found. [FFmpeg tables](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/doc/general_contents.texi), [RoQ encoder](https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/blob/master/libavcodec/roqvideoenc.c) |

## When downscaling helps

Calculated pixel counts: 3840x2160 to 1920x1080 removes 75% of pixels; 2560x1440 to 1080p removes 43.75%. Neither predicts the same percentage reduction in compressed bytes. Bitrate/quality settings, content, audio and packaging determine the result. At unchanged total bitrate and duration, shrinking resolution alone provides little file-size benefit. Measure representative output, visual quality and in-game playback before estimating savings. Preserve frame count/rate, duration, aspect ratio, colour/HDR, transparency, language tracks, subtitles and cue points wherever required. The agreed feature skips already-1080p/lower-resolution sources.

Archives such as `.archive`, `.pak`, `.bundle` or `.cpk` are packaging, not video codecs. Smaller encoded video saves installed space only with a validated archive rewrite/compaction path when embedded. Cyberpunk's inspected main video archive contained Bink but no qualifying 4K headers; that finding does not cover every archive. [Local inspection](cyberpunk-video-compatibility.md)

Backups delay net local savings: retaining the full original on the same volume plus converted output initially increases usage. Preview files also consume space. Account separately for temporary output, rebuilt archives and backup retention. [Current backup implementation](video-pipeline.md)

## Licensing and recommendation

RAD explicitly requires a licence for commercial Bink encoder use. CRI documents authenticated tools and commercial licensing arrangements. Verify encoder access and integration/redistribution terms for GameZip's intended distribution rather than inferring them from a game's bundled decoder. Smacker tool distribution terms also need review. [RAD FAQ](https://www.radgametools.com/binkfaq.htm), [CRI tool authentication](https://www.criware.cn/sdk_manual/SOFDEC_pc/contents/usr_tools_env.html), [CRI product/licensing](https://game.criware.jp/products/sofdec/)

Prioritize a real 4K standalone H.264 MP4 game profile, then WebM or MPEG/Theora where an actual title justifies it. Treat proprietary families as research candidates pending lawful encoder access, full metadata preservation, packaging and runtime validation. FFmpeg build/dependency distribution obligations remain separate from GameZip's MIT licence. [Existing distribution checkpoint](video-pipeline.md)
